# CureAR

CureAR is an AI-enabled medical billing and revenue cycle management platform built for modern healthcare practices.

It brings billing workflows, claims, payments, denials, reporting, and revenue insights into one place. The goal is simple: help healthcare teams spend less time dealing with repetitive billing work and have a clearer view of what is happening with their revenue cycle.

CureAR is developed by TechMatter.

**Website:** https://curear.com

**Product:** https://app.curear.com/

## What CureAR Does

Medical billing involves a lot of moving parts. Claims need to be prepared and checked, submitted to payers, tracked, corrected when necessary, and followed through until payment is received.

CureAR helps organize these workflows and uses automation and AI-assisted capabilities to reduce manual work.

The platform can help teams with:

* Claims management
* Claim validation and scrubbing
* Denial management
* Payment reconciliation
* Prior authorization workflows
* Revenue cycle reporting
* Financial dashboards and analytics
* Payer and claim visibility
* Billing workflow automation
* Compliance-focused processes
* EHR and practice management integrations

The exact features and workflows available to an organization depend on its configuration and implementation.

## Why CureAR

Most billing teams don't need another complicated system. They need better visibility and fewer things to manage manually.

CureAR is designed around that idea.

Instead of relying on disconnected spreadsheets, manual follow-ups, and multiple sources of billing information, teams can use a centralized platform to monitor their revenue cycle and act on issues as they appear.

The platform provides real-time dashboards and reporting that can help teams understand areas such as claims, denials, collections, accounts receivable, and payer performance.

## Key Capabilities

### Claims

Manage claims throughout the billing cycle and identify issues that may affect successful processing.

### Denial Management

Track denied claims, understand common causes, and give billing teams a clearer path toward correction and follow-up.

### Payment Reconciliation

Match payments with billing records and reduce the manual effort involved in keeping financial information accurate.

### Prior Authorization

Organize authorization workflows and reduce the need for repeated manual follow-ups.

### Reporting and Analytics

Use dashboards, reports, trends, and key revenue cycle metrics to understand how a practice is performing.

### Automation

Automate repetitive parts of the billing workflow so staff can spend more time on work that requires human attention.

### Integrations

CureAR is designed to work alongside existing healthcare systems rather than forcing practices to replace everything they already use.

## Built for Different Healthcare Organizations

CureAR can support different practice sizes and billing environments, from smaller practices to multi-location healthcare organizations.

It can also support medical billing companies managing revenue cycle workflows for their clients.

The right setup depends on the organization's specialty, systems, payer mix, and operational requirements.
