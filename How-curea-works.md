# How CureAR Works

CureAR connects the different steps involved in the healthcare revenue cycle so billing teams can manage their work from a central platform.

The exact workflow can vary by organization, specialty, payer mix, and system configuration. At a high level, the process looks like this:

```text
Healthcare Systems
       ↓
Patient & Visit Data
       ↓
Billing & Coding
       ↓
Claim Validation
       ↓
Claim Submission
       ↓
Payer Processing
       ↓
Payments / Denials
       ↓
Reconciliation
       ↓
Reporting & Revenue Insights
```

## 1. Connect Existing Systems

CureAR is designed to work with existing healthcare technology rather than requiring a practice to start from scratch.

Depending on the implementation, information can be exchanged with EHR, practice management, clearinghouse, and other connected systems.

The goal is to bring the information needed for billing into a workflow that is easier to manage.

## 2. Prepare Billing Information

Once the relevant information is available, billing workflows can begin.

This may include reviewing patient and visit information, charges, coding details, payer information, and other data required for a claim.

CureAR's automation and AI-assisted capabilities can help identify information that needs attention.

## 3. Validate Claims

Before a claim is submitted, it can go through validation and claim-scrubbing processes.

The system is designed to identify issues that could cause a claim to be rejected or denied.

Finding these problems earlier can save billing teams from unnecessary rework later in the process.

## 4. Submit and Track Claims

After validation, claims can move through the submission workflow.

CureAR provides visibility into claim status so teams can see what has been submitted, what is still pending, and what requires attention.

This gives billing staff a single place to monitor claim activity instead of relying on disconnected tracking methods.

## 5. Work Denials and Exceptions

Not every claim moves through the system without a problem.

When a claim is rejected or denied, the billing team needs to understand what happened and decide what to do next.

CureAR supports denial workflows that help organize these cases and identify recurring patterns.

Over time, denial reporting can show where problems are happening most often.

## 6. Track Payments

When payments are received, they need to be recorded and matched with the appropriate billing information.

CureAR provides payment and reconciliation workflows that help teams maintain an accurate view of collections and outstanding balances.

## 7. Understand Performance

The final step is not simply collecting money.

Teams need to understand how the entire revenue cycle is performing.

CureAR provides dashboards and reporting that can help users review claims, denials, collections, accounts receivable, payer activity, and revenue trends.

These insights can then be used to improve the workflow.

## The Result

The idea behind CureAR is straightforward:

**Connect the data. Reduce manual work. Catch problems earlier. Understand the revenue cycle.**

Rather than treating billing as a collection of separate tasks, CureAR brings those tasks into a connected workflow.

That gives billing teams and practice leaders a clearer view of what is happening and where they should focus next.
