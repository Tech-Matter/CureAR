# CureAR Integrations

Healthcare organizations rarely operate with a single system.

An EHR may hold clinical information. A practice management system may handle scheduling and patient administration. A clearinghouse may handle claim transmission. Other systems may manage payments, reporting, or specific parts of the revenue cycle.

CureAR is designed to fit into this existing environment.

## EHR and Practice Management Systems

CureAR can integrate with existing EHR and practice management environments to support billing workflows.

This allows organizations to keep the systems they already depend on while connecting relevant information to their revenue cycle operations.

Integration requirements vary depending on the system, available interfaces, and the organization's workflow.

## Clearinghouse Connectivity

Claims often move through clearinghouses before reaching payers.

CureAR supports clearinghouse-based workflows to help billing teams manage claim submission and related status information.

The specific clearinghouse connection available to an organization depends on its implementation.

## Data Exchange

Healthcare data needs to move between systems accurately and securely.

Depending on the integration, CureAR can work with structured healthcare and billing data used throughout the revenue cycle.

Integration methods and data formats can vary by system and use case.

## APIs and Custom Integrations

Organizations with specific technical requirements may need custom integration work.

CureAR can support integration planning around an organization's existing technology environment and operational needs.

For integration discussions, contact the CureAR team to review available options.

## Integration Goals

A good integration should do more than move data from one system to another.

The goal is to reduce duplicate work and give billing teams access to the information they need without constantly switching between systems.

Typical integration goals include:

* Reducing manual data entry
* Keeping billing information synchronized
* Improving claim visibility
* Supporting automated workflows
* Connecting payment information
* Improving reporting
* Reducing operational friction

## Implementation

Every healthcare organization has a different setup.

Before an integration is implemented, the CureAR team can review the organization's systems, workflows, data requirements, security considerations, and operational goals.

Implementation may include:

1. Reviewing the existing technology environment
2. Identifying the data that needs to move between systems
3. Defining the required workflows
4. Configuring the integration
5. Testing data exchange
6. Validating the billing workflow
7. Supporting the team during rollout

## Existing Technology Matters

CureAR is not intended to force every practice into the same technology stack.

A smaller practice and a multi-location healthcare organization may have very different integration requirements.

The best approach is to understand the existing environment first and then determine how CureAR should fit into it.
