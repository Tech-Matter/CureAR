# CureAR Use Cases

CureAR can be used in different healthcare revenue cycle environments.

The platform is flexible because billing problems are rarely identical from one organization to another. A small practice may need better claim visibility, while a larger organization may be dealing with thousands of claims, multiple locations, and a much more complex payer mix.

Here are some common ways CureAR can fit into a revenue cycle operation.

## Medical Practices

Small and mid-sized practices often have limited billing resources.

When the same people are responsible for billing, follow-ups, reporting, and other administrative work, manual processes can quickly become a problem.

CureAR can help centralize billing workflows, monitor claims, track payments, and give practice leaders a clearer view of revenue cycle performance.

## Multi-Location Practices

Managing billing across several locations introduces another layer of complexity.

Leadership may need to compare performance between locations while billing teams need to manage a larger volume of claims.

CureAR's centralized dashboards and reporting can help organizations monitor billing activity across their operations.

## Medical Billing Companies

Billing companies manage revenue cycle operations for multiple healthcare clients.

That means they need visibility not only into individual claims, but also into client-level performance.

CureAR can support billing companies with centralized workflows, automation, reporting, claim management, denial tracking, and revenue cycle analytics.

The goal is to help billing teams manage more work without adding the same amount of manual effort.

## Healthcare Provider Groups

Larger provider groups often have more complicated billing environments.

Different specialties, locations, payers, and workflows can make it difficult to maintain consistent processes.

CureAR can provide a shared revenue cycle environment where teams can monitor claims, denials, payments, and financial performance.

## Practices Dealing With High Denial Rates

Denials can create a large amount of rework.

Teams need to know which claims were denied, why they were denied, and whether the same issues are happening repeatedly.

CureAR's denial workflows and reporting can help teams organize this work and identify patterns that deserve attention.

## Practices With Manual Billing Workflows

Spreadsheets and manual tracking can work for a while.

Eventually, they can become difficult to maintain as claim volume increases.

CureAR can help move billing operations into a centralized digital workflow with automation, dashboards, and reporting.

## Practices Looking for Better Revenue Visibility

Sometimes the problem is not a lack of data. It is the lack of a clear view of that data.

CureAR provides dashboards and reporting that help users monitor revenue cycle activity and key performance indicators.

This can help practice leaders make decisions based on current operational information rather than waiting for a monthly report.

## Practices Improving Payment Reconciliation

Payment reconciliation can take significant time when teams have to manually match transactions and billing records.

CureAR provides workflows designed to make reconciliation more organized and reduce unnecessary manual effort.

## Organizations Exploring AI in RCM

AI can be useful when applied to the right parts of the revenue cycle.

CureAR uses AI-assisted capabilities alongside automation and reporting to help teams handle repetitive work and identify areas that may need attention.

The objective is practical: use technology to help billing professionals work more efficiently while keeping people involved where judgment is required.

## Specialty-Specific Workflows

Different specialties have different billing requirements.

A laboratory, behavioral health organization, physician practice, or other healthcare provider may have its own coding, authorization, payer, and workflow considerations.

CureAR can be configured around the needs of different healthcare environments.
