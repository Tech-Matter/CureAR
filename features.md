# CureAR Features

CureAR brings the main parts of the revenue cycle into a connected workflow.

The platform is designed to reduce repetitive work, give billing teams better visibility, and make it easier to spot problems before they turn into larger revenue issues.

## Claims Management

Claims are at the center of most revenue cycle workflows.

CureAR helps teams manage claims from preparation through submission and follow-up. Claim workflows can include validation, status tracking, corrections, and visibility into what happens after submission.

The goal is to reduce unnecessary rework and give billing teams a clearer picture of their claim pipeline.

## Claim Scrubbing

Errors discovered after a claim reaches a payer can create delays and additional work.

CureAR provides claim-scrubbing capabilities designed to identify issues before submission. Depending on the workflow, this can include coding problems, missing information, and payer-specific edits.

Early validation gives billing teams a chance to fix issues before they become avoidable denials or rejections.

## Denial Management

Denials can quickly become expensive when they are handled manually.

CureAR helps teams track denied claims, organize follow-up, and identify patterns across their billing data.

Understanding why claims are being denied is just as important as working the individual denial. Reporting and analytics can help teams identify recurring problems and prioritize where attention is needed.

## Payment Reconciliation

Getting paid is only part of the process. Payments also need to be matched correctly with the related billing information.

CureAR provides payment reconciliation workflows that help teams keep payment records organized and reduce manual matching.

This can improve visibility into outstanding balances and make financial reporting easier to manage.

## Prior Authorization

Prior authorization can involve repeated communication, documentation, and follow-up.

CureAR supports authorization workflows designed to help teams organize this work and keep track of authorization-related activity.

The objective is to reduce unnecessary administrative effort while keeping time-sensitive requests visible.

## Revenue Reporting

CureAR provides dashboards and reporting tools for monitoring revenue cycle performance.

Teams can review metrics such as:

* Claims submitted
* Claims pending
* Claims paid
* Denial activity
* Accounts receivable
* Collection performance
* Payer performance
* Revenue trends

Reports can help practice leaders move from simply looking at numbers to understanding where action may be needed.

## Revenue Intelligence

Raw billing data does not always tell the full story.

CureAR uses analytics and AI-assisted capabilities to surface patterns and potential revenue opportunities within billing workflows.

This can help teams focus their time on areas that are more likely to have an impact instead of manually reviewing every record.

## Workflow Automation

Billing teams spend a significant amount of time on repetitive administrative tasks.

CureAR is designed to automate parts of these workflows where appropriate. Automation can help reduce repetitive data handling, follow-ups, and manual checks.

The intent is not to remove people from the process. It is to give billing professionals better tools and more time for work that needs human judgment.

## Cloud-Based Access

CureAR is a cloud-based platform, allowing authorized users to access the system without depending on a traditional on-premise billing environment.

This approach also makes it easier for organizations with multiple locations or distributed teams to work from a shared system.

## Compliance-Focused Workflows

Healthcare billing operates under strict regulatory and payer requirements.

CureAR includes compliance-focused capabilities and safeguards designed to support healthcare billing operations.

Organizations should always review their own compliance responsibilities and implementation requirements with their appropriate compliance and legal teams.

## Built to Scale

Billing requirements can change as a practice grows.

CureAR is designed to support different practice sizes and operational models, including smaller practices, multi-location organizations, and medical billing companies.

The platform can be configured around the organization's workflows rather than requiring every customer to operate in exactly the same way.
